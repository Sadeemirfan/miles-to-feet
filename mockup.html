How to Convert Miles to Feet

Converting miles to feet is very simple. Since one mile contains exactly 5,280 feet, you multiply the number of miles by the conversion factor to complete the conversion.

The Conversion Formula

Multiply miles by the standard ratio:

Feet = Miles × 5,280

Example Calculation

Converting 5 miles into feet:

5 × 5,280 = 26,400 ft

Frequently Asked Questions

How many feet are in a mile?

There are exactly 5,280 feet in one standard statute mile.

How do you convert miles to feet manually?

Take the total number of miles and multiply it by 5,280. For example, 3 miles × 5,280 = 15,840 feet.

Why are there 5,280 feet in a mile?

This length was standardized in 1593 under Queen Elizabeth I, combining the traditional agricultural furlong (660 feet) with the Roman mile measurement (8 furlongs = 5,280 feet).
