How Feet to Miles Works

The calculation is simple. There are exactly 5,280 feet in one mile. To convert any distance in feet to miles, divide the number of feet by 5,280.

The Formula

Divide feet by the conversion factor:

Miles = Feet ÷ 5,280

Example Calculation

Convert 10,560 feet to miles:

10,560 ÷ 5,280 = 2 mi
