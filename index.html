How to Convert Miles to Feet

Converting distance between miles (mi) and feet (ft) is a simple mathematical multiplication. Since the mile is a larger unit of length than the foot, you multiply the total number of miles by the standard conversion factor of 5,280.

The Conversion Formula

Multiply miles by 5,280 to get the length in feet:

Feet = Miles × 5,280

Formula in Action

For example, to convert 5 miles into feet:

5 × 5,280 = 26,400 ft

The History Behind the 5,280-Foot Statute Mile

Have you ever wondered why there are exactly 5,280 feet in a mile instead of a round number like 5,000 or 10,000? The answer lies in historical convergence between ancient Roman systems and traditional English agricultural measurements.

The word "mile" originates from the Latin phrase "mille passus", which translates to "a thousand paces." In the ancient Roman measurement system, a single pace represented a double-step (approximately 5 Roman feet). Thus, a Roman mile was defined as exactly 5,000 Roman feet.

In medieval England, however, farmers utilized the furlong (meaning "furrow-long," which was the distance an ox team could plow a field before needing to rest, standardized as 660 feet). Because farmers, surveyors, and the English parliament sought to consolidate land boundaries without disrupting agriculture, Queen Elizabeth I passed a statute in 1593.

This act officially standardized the English Statute Mile as being equal to exactly 8 furlongs. Multiplying 8 furlongs by 660 feet yields 5,280 feet, cementing this odd measurement system in the Imperial and US customary units we use today.

Unit Definitions: Mile vs Foot

What is a Mile (mi)?

A mile (mi) is a unit of length in the imperial and US customary measurement systems. One statute mile equals exactly 5,280 feet, 1,760 yards, or 1.609344 kilometers (1,609.344 meters).

  • Origin: Roman mille passus (1,000 double paces / 5,000 Roman feet)
  • Statute Standard: Established in 1593 under Queen Elizabeth I (8 furlongs × 660 ft)
  • Primary Use: Road travel, geography, land surveying, and marathon athletics

What is a Foot (ft)?

A foot (ft) is a unit of length equal to exactly 12 inches, 0.3333 yards, or 0.3048 meters. The foot is one of the most common units for measuring height, room dimensions, and construction scaling.

  • Reverse Conversion: 1 foot = 0.000189394 miles (1 ÷ 5,280)
  • Metric Ratio: Exactly 304.8 millimeters (30.48 cm)
  • Primary Use: Building plans, architecture, elevation, and personal height
